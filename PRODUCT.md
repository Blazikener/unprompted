# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Agency partnerships leads (YKONE-style influencer agencies in the GCC) who have to put a creator shortlist in front of a brand client. They work on a laptop in a bright office, and present the same screen on a projector. The job: find creators who already love the brand, and prove it with evidence the client can't argue with.

## Product Purpose
Unprompted listens to creator videos through Oriane's transcript and frame index and finds the moments a brand is said out loud, including mentions that never reach a caption or hashtag (invisible to caption-based social listening). It ranks those creators as ready-made ambassadors, shows the exact quote, timestamp and on-screen frame, and drafts outreach that quotes the moment back. Success: a partnerships lead goes from brand name to a defensible, evidence-backed shortlist in under a minute.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, credible, fast. Evidence-first: quotes, timestamps and numbers do the persuading, not adjectives. Calm confidence of a research tool, never hype.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS admin templates: purple gradients, identical card grids, big-number hero-metric tiles.
- Anything that decorates instead of proving.

## Design Principles
- Show the receipt: every claim links to the moment in the video that backs it.
- The hidden mention is the hero: surface what caption tools miss.
- One glance, one decision: the shortlist and its evidence live on one screen.
- Explain the score: rankings are transparent, never a black box.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, full keyboard access, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Arabic and English content side by side (`dir="auto"` on user content).
