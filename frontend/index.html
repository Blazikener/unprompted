        How it works

        1. 01

          Search your brand once

          Type the name and how people say it out loud. We read the last 12 months of creator videos on Instagram and TikTok.

        2. 02

          Meet your advocates

          Creators ranked by how often, how recently and to how many people they said your name, with the quote and the frame for every mention.

        3. 03

          Hear the next ones first

          Press Watch weekly. Every 7 days you get only the new videos by email. No new mentions, no email.

        4. 04

          Invite them to collab

          Propose new content in their style: what they make, when it posts, the budget. They accept or counter, and the collab is booked.
