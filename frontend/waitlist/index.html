Blind

Cold

Our rules

Proof, not vibes.

Verbatim or nothing

Every claim links to the second it was said. If we can't show it, we don't count it.

Public videos only

We read public transcripts. No logins, no DMs, nothing posted for you.

Ads kept apart

Disclosed sponsorships are labelled as ads, never passed off as organic love.

You approve it

No pitch, licence or payment moves without the creator saying yes.

Questions

Before you join.

When do I get in?

Invites go out in waves, earliest spots first. Inviting friends moves you up 5 spots each. You'll get one email when your invite is ready.

Is it free?

Joining the waitlist is free and needs no card. Creators will be able to scan for free and see their top brands; deeper features are a paid plan. Early members hear the pricing first.

Which platforms?

TikTok and Instagram today. Tell us in step 2 if you mostly post somewhere else; that's exactly the signal we use to decide what's next.

Do you post or DM for me?

Never. Receipts reads public video transcripts. Pitches are drafts you send yourself, and licence offers need your yes.

What happens to my email?

It's used for your invite and launch updates from us, nothing else. It's never sold or shared.
