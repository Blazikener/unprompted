003 / Both sides of the deal

You don't go hunting. Brands that watch their own name find you.

If you create

  • A receipt for every mention. Quote, timestamp, frame and views, on a public page you can drop into a pitch or media kit.
  • An inbox for licence offers. A brand asks to run one of your videos as a paid ad for 30 days. You see the price, then accept, counter or decline. Set a minimum and auto-yes or auto-no brands, and requests answer themselves.
  • You keep ownership, and 85%. The brand pays before the ad goes live. You never post anything new, and nothing is exclusive.
  • A weekly email, only when something needs you: offers waiting, licences ending, money owed. Never an empty one.

If you're a brand

  • Find the creators already saying your name. Ranked as advocates, with the exact quote and frame, including mentions no caption or hashtag tool sees.
  • Watch weekly. Save a search and get a report of new on-camera mentions each week.
  • License a clip in one click. Request a 30-day Spark Ad or partnership-ad licence with the price up front. The creator consents, so there's no chasing and no unlicensed reuse.
  • Month-to-month, no contracts. Pay per licence, and renew with one click a week before it ends.
